# Reed Instrument Making: Acoustics, Construction and Practice

This repository contains a collection of technical documents and calculation scripts focused on the acoustics, construction, and physics of reed instruments, such as accordions and harmonicas.

## Overview
The provided materials bridge the gap between traditional craftsmanship and modern engineering. By applying principles from electronics and communications technology—such as frequency analysis, impedance matching, and resonant circuits—these documents offer a rigorous framework for understanding reed acoustics.

### Key Topics Covered:
* **Acoustics & Flow:** Analysis of reed flow (e.g., 50 Hz bass reeds) and frequency variation through chamber coupling.
* **Chamber & Tone:** How chamber geometry affects tone modification and the coupling of multiple reeds.
* **Reed & Plate Mechanics:** Quality factors, channel geometry, and reed stiffness.
* **Tuning & Tremolo:** The physics of beats, difference tones, and tuning practice.
* **Construction:** Technical notes on bellows, cabinets, and reed plate manufacturing.

## Python Scripts
To verify the numerical values and physical models presented in the collection, this repository includes Python calculation scripts. These tools allow makers and researchers to verify acoustic models independently and calculate chamber frequencies based on specific dimensions.

## About the Author

With over 25 years of experience in building and repairing reed instruments, he combines analytical engineering with traditional workshop practice learned from experienced masters.

## Document Structure
The collection is organized into specific document IDs (e.g., Dok. 0002 for Flow Analysis). Each document addresses a specific topic that arose from practical questions in the workshop.
